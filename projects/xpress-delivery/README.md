# Xpress Delivery

> Technology-enabled same-day delivery platform built around a distributed driver network and enterprise e-commerce integrations.

**Period:** 2018–2025  
**Role:** co-founder / CEO-CVO, product and technology operator  
**Scale:** 61 Polish cities + Prague at historical scale; ~20 people hired; ~PLN 3M external capital raised  
**Strategic investor:** CCC Group

## What it was

Xpress Delivery was not simply a courier company. The operating model depended on software: a driver-facing application, dispatch and order orchestration, pricing/operations logic, and integrations that allowed enterprise e-commerce systems to request and manage same-day delivery.

The product direction borrowed from the marketplace logic popularized by platforms such as Uber/Bolt, but applied it to last-mile delivery for merchants and enterprise customers.

## My role

I worked across business, product and technology rather than treating them as separate domains.

- identified operational and customer problems and translated them into product requirements;
- designed core system and workflow concepts for driver operations, dispatch and enterprise integrations;
- led software-development priorities and delivery with development teams;
- hired ~20 people across the organization;
- worked directly with enterprise customers, partners and investors;
- raised ~PLN 3M in external capital;
- secured CCC Group as a strategic investor;
- operated the system at multi-city scale and used real operational feedback to shape the product.

## What I learned

Xpress shaped how I build AI products today. Software only matters when it survives contact with operations: imperfect inputs, distributed users, exceptions, customer expectations, financial constraints and real-world consequences.

That is why my current AI work starts with process and operating reality rather than with a model looking for a use case.
