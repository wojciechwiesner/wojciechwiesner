# EcoXpress

> High-scale ride-hailing fleet operations supported by internally designed driver and settlement systems.

**Period:** 2016–2022  
**Role:** founder/operator, systems designer and technology lead  
**Scale:** ~14,000-driver network at peak operating scale

## Business problem

Running a large distributed driver network creates a systems problem long before it becomes a software problem: onboarding, driver status, operational support, settlements, reconciliation, exceptions and large volumes of repetitive administrative work.

## What I owned

- ran the operation at large driver-network scale;
- designed the internal driver settlement/accounting system;
- translated operational requirements into software workflows and priorities;
- coordinated development work around the systems needed by the operation;
- used software to reduce manual work and make a distributed physical operation manageable at scale.

## System view

```text
driver activity / platform data
            ↓
normalization + business rules
            ↓
settlement / accounting logic
            ↓
exceptions + reconciliation
            ↓
driver-facing / operator-facing outputs
```

## Why this matters to my work today

EcoXpress was where I learned to treat operational complexity as a system-design problem. Years before working directly with modern LLMs, I was already looking for the same thing I look for now: where human work can be decomposed into state, rules, data and repeatable execution — and where software should support judgment instead of pretending exceptions do not exist.
